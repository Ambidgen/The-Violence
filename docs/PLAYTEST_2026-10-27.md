# Playtest — The Violence 1.3 candidate (roguelike story mode)

2026-10-27. Played on the official Godot 4.7.2 engine (Linux, software GL under a virtual
display) and through the headless harnesses. Also checked the exported Windows build's
pack, which boots clean. Two passes:
**round 1** on the 1.21 tree as received, **round 2** after the run layer went in,
Endless Battle came out, and the round-1 fixes landed.

## How it was played

- **By hand:** title → new run → inheritance → procurement → a hostage rescue → end of
  week → training-program draft → an event → the armoury screen → the Force screen and
  kit editor. In round 1: two operations (a barricade and a gang house), internal
  affairs, end of week.
- **Headless, 40 weeks each, seed 1001, sensible chief** (`op_test.gd --chief=sensible`):
  the 1.21 classic campaign; the run layer with the desk answered at random, before the
  fixes; and the run layer after the fixes.
- **Suites:** quick tier 10/10; full tier 18 pass, 3 notes; `orders_test` passes alone
  in 348 s but times out at 600 s under load. Determinism holds: 35/35 cases reproduce.
- **Probe:** `tests/event_probe.gd` (new) — two officers on post at a quiet rally,
  varying trauma, morale and habits.

## What round 2 found

1. **A trauma cliff at public events.** Two officers posted at a quiet rally: at trauma
   10, no civilian is hit in four events; at trauma 14, **19 are killed**. (Morale 30
   alone does nothing.) Past a stress floor of about 0.2, officers misjudge someone in
   the crowd, fire, the crowd panics, and the fear feeds itself for fifteen minutes.
   This drove the before-fixes run: a desk that lost the union lowered morale, the
   rallies turned into massacres, and the lawsuits sank the account. *Mitigated:*
   Suggest a plan now keeps officers at trauma 12+ off crowd duty, and the Force screen
   flags them. *Not fixed:* the cliff is still in the simulation.
2. **Civilians hit were counted by the round.** One bystander struck three times was
   "3 civilians hit" in the record, "Bystanders shot" in the Ledger, and three lawsuits'
   worth of damages. One settlement reached **$310,000**. *Fixed:* civilians are now
   counted as people.
3. **The force bleeds out.** Every 40-week run ends with 5–7 fit officers of 20, 11–12
   killed, and 55–62 situations left unattended. The sensible chief never hires, but the
   game never pushes them to: academy places take two weeks, and nothing warns that the
   roster can't cover next week's board.
4. **The money curve is upside down.** Accounts go negative in weeks 4–16 (hiring and
   kit frozen exactly when they are needed), then pile up past $200,000 by election
   night with nothing to spend it on.
5. **Allies collapse early and stay lost.** By week 20 the council, union and coalition
   are below 25 in every run, and win-back (above 40) almost never happens. Losing them
   is a one-way door.
6. **The desk works and reads well.** Drafts, offers and events showed correct costs and
   effects. The armoury gate fell back cleanly (no flashbangs until they're drafted), and
   the inheritance gate held the first operation. One defect in play: the kit
   editor's drop-downs pushed the panel off the screen. *Fixed.*
7. **Management screens waste two-thirds of the screen.** Board, desk, IA, budget and
   roster draw on the right 460 px. The left shows a test-range map unrelated to the
   screen, or nothing.
8. **The first operation spends money on its own.** The guided first operation applies
   Suggest a plan, kit included, and $4,700 leaves the account before the player has
   touched anything.
9. **The harnesses can lie.** During development `_check.gd` printed parse errors and
   still reported "0 failed". `orders_test` needs more than its 600 s budget on a busy
   two-core runner.
10. **Still open from release readiness:** 10 of 14 cutscene shorts are never played;
    there is no music; no licence file; the Windows exe has never been run on Windows
    hardware and is unsigned.

## Ten recommendations to get to a release

1. **Remove the trauma cliff in the simulation, not just in the plan.** In
   `world.gd misjudge_p()` / `add_stress()`, cap a misjudgement-triggered escalation in
   a crowd: after an officer fires at someone later judged a civilian, force the
   **rally reset** (Q1) for that officer and their squad for 20 s, and halve stress gain
   from crowd panic events. Gate: `event_probe.gd --trauma=14 --n=8` must show ≤ 1
   civilian hit per event, and `--trauma=0` unchanged.
2. **Make the roster a live constraint the player can see.** Add a "next week" strip to
   the board: fit officers versus the want of the open situations (`Autoplan.want_for`),
   and a warning below 12 fit. Let the chief buy an **emergency lateral** that reports
   the same week at 2× cost. Gate: `op_test --chief=sensible --hire` keeps 12 or more fit
   through week 40 on three seeds.
3. **Fix the money curve.** Start the reserve at $35,000. Charge for kit through the
   week's overtime and not at assignment. Add late-game sinks with real effects: a
   **paid desk** (contracts and programs at tier 3 cost more), a capital item
   (armoured rescue vehicle, a training range), and **union contracts** that raise pay
   and morale_home. Gate: no run below $0 in weeks 1–12, and no run above $100,000 at
   week 40, across five `op_test --rogue` seeds.
4. **Give lost allies a way back.** Every offer from a lost ally should be a
   **reconciliation**: big cost, standing +20 on acceptance. At most one such offer every
   four weeks. Gate: in five autoplayed runs, at least one ally lost and won back.
5. **Use the left side of the management screens.** Draw the city: a ward map with trust,
   this week's situations as pins, and the desk's people as portraits. During the
   overhaul this becomes the child's drawing of Port Carrick. Remove the test-range map
   from the board.
6. **Make the first operation's spending explicit.** In `ops_select` on the guided
   first operation, apply squads and orders but not paid kit. Show "Suggested kit:
   $4,700 — Issue" as a button.
7. **Make every harness fail honestly.** `_check.gd` should count a failed `load()` or
   a script with `can_instantiate() == false` as a failure. Give all 18 diagnostics an
   exit code. Scale `run_tests.sh` timeouts by `nproc`. Move `tools/ci/ci.yml` into
   `.github/workflows/`.
8. **Balance the run layer with numbers, not taste.** Add `rogue_balance_test.gd`: 20
   seeds × 40 weeks with the desk answered by four fixed policies (always accept /
   always decline / restraint-first / military-first). Record score, deaths, allies and
   money per policy, and assert that no single policy dominates on all four.
9. **Finish the content that ships half-wired.** Wire the remaining ten shorts at the
   beats `cinematics/SHORTS.md` names, or drop them from the export (8.6 MB). Add one
   music bed per screen family: title, board, operation, debrief.
10. **Ship it properly.** Rebuild the single-file exe from this tree and run it on a real
    Windows 10/11 machine (boot, a week, save, reload, alt-tab, resize, quit). Add
    `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` and settle the Remotion licence. Code-sign or
    publish through itch.io with the SmartScreen note. Bump to **1.3.0** with
    `tools/sync_version.py`.
