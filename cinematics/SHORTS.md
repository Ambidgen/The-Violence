# The short cutscenes

Generated by `node scripts/manifest.mjs` from `src/shorts/registry.js`. Do not edit by hand.

Every short runs 5-10 s at 30 fps. Native runtime scenes are drawn in Godot from the same
crayon page as the game: toned paper, charcoal hatching, hand-drawn lettering, colour with purpose.
Each one is prop-driven: the plates are drawn, the words come from the campaign. Where a
`slots` entry is listed, the engine may also paint its own text over an empty plate instead
of asking for a re-render.

## S01-Week · The Board Refills

The week number and the calendar, then this week's situations dealt onto the board.

- **Length:** 180 frames, 6.000 s
- **Fires on:** Start of every campaign week, before the board is shown
- **Source:** `src/shorts/S01_WeekCard.jsx`
- **Sound:** amb_office bed, click, stamp, toll at the end

| prop | type | default |
|------|------|---------|
| `week` | int | `12` | Campaign week, stamped large and lit on the calendar.
| `of` | int | `40` | Total weeks; the calendar has this many cells.
| `ward` | string | `"Ironworks"` | The ward the week opens on (one of the six).
| `cards` | array | `[{"kind":"Warrant service","ward":"Northgate"},{"kind":"Gang house raid","ward":"Kilbride"}]` | Up to three {kind, ward} slips; the board deals them.
| `note` | string | `"TWO SITUATIONS ON THE BOARD"` | The line under the week number.
| `stamp` | array | `["WEEK 12"]` | Lines for the rubber stamp.
| `showSlots` | boolean | `false` | 

Slots the engine may overpaint: `week-title` (A short engine-written line under the numeral (e.g. the ward).); `note` (One line of body text over the calendar.).
